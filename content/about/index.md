Welcome! I'm an ocean and climate scientist working on marine carbon dioxide removal (mCDR). I completed my Ph.D. at UC Santa Cruz, where I specialized in carbonate chemistry and ocean biogeochemical modeling. My research focused on [ocean alkalinity enhancement (OAE)](https://www.dosi-project.org/wp-content/uploads/Alkalinity-Enhancement-Policy-Brief.pdf) as a tool for [marine carbon dioxide removal (mCDR)](https://oceanvisions.org/ocean-based-carbon-dioxide-removal/), using carbon isotope tracers and ocean models to evaluate the feasibility and traceability of ocean-based CDR.

My projects spanned geologic analogs from the last deglaciation to high-resolution simulations of future OAE scenarios in the California Current System. A key thread throughout my work has been the use of carbon isotopes to understand and verify carbon cycle dynamics—past, present, and future.

I now work full time at [Equatic](https://www.equatic.tech/), where I lead our MRV framework, life-cycle assessment, and environmental science for our marine carbon dioxide removal plants. I built our MRV framework, Project Design Document, and data infrastructure for our demonstration plant in Singapore, and I'm now working across GHG accounting, environmental monitoring, permitting, and environmental risk assessment as we move toward larger-scale commercial deployments.

When I’m not working, I enjoy being outside—playing sports, hiking, or spending time in the ocean—and unwinding by trying new recipes in the kitchen.
